It's official: **pre-orders for the Picco** will open **in early 2027**, at a preferential price of **€350**.

## The Picco, in short

The Picco is our next Newtonian telescope, **114/450**, designed to push nomadic astronomy even further than the Smallest: just **1.5 kg**, an ultra-compact build, and the same DNA: an instrument that makes sense the moment you pick it up, no manual required.

Release planned for **early 2027**, at the same time pre-orders open. That will be your chance to reserve yours right at launch, at the launch price.

## Why a preferential price

The €350 pre-order price rewards those who've been following along from the start and trust the project before its official release. The final price, once the Picco is available in the shop, will be announced later, and it will be higher.

## How to be notified

The exact pre-order opening date will be announced on this blog and on our social media. If you want to make sure you don't miss it, the simplest way is to [email us](mailto:la3emedim@gmail.com) to be notified first, or follow the [Picco product page](/en/product/picco/), which will be updated as soon as pre-orders open.

See you soon for the next steps!
