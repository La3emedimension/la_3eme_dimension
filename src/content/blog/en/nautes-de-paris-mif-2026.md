Paris website **Les Nautes de Paris** spent a day in the **Crafts Village** at the [Made in France show](/en/blog/salon-made-in-france-2026/), which brought together 210 craftspeople in Paris from November 12 to 15, 2026. In their report, La 3ème Dimension appears in the "Environment" section, under the heading "Looking beyond with La 3e Dimension".

## What the article covers

The article notes that the **[Lumi educational refractor kit](/en/product/lumi/)** made its debut at the show and drew parents and children to the stand. It describes it as a 3D-printed educational model you assemble yourself, already being demonstrated in a few schools in the region.

It also mentions the workshop's two telescopes: the **[Smallest](/en/product/smallest/)**, with its carbon tubes, which folds down to about twenty centimeters, and the lighter **[Picco](/en/product/picco/)**, currently being finalized.

The report also features other craftspeople from the village: guitar and trumpet makers, a cardboard furniture maker, a featherwork jeweler, a knife maker, a chocolatier, and Diptyque Audio, another Tarn-et-Garonne company.
