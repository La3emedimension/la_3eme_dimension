The **Rencontres du Ciel et de l'Espace (RCE)**, France's major astronomy event organized by the French Astronomy Association (AFA), Ciel & Espace and the Cité des sciences et de l'industrie, returns for its 15th edition from **November 13 to 15, 2026** in Paris.

On the program: more than 150 speakers, planetarium shows, a second-hand equipment sale and the **astronomy show**, over 1,000 m² dedicated to manufacturers, retailers, associations and laboratories.

## La 3ème Dimension on the La Clef des Étoiles booth

This year, **La 3ème Dimension** instruments are presented by **[La Clef des Étoiles](https://www.laclefdesetoiles.com)** (Cosmodiff), **booths 49 to 54**, level S2. It's a chance to see and try the **[Smallest](/en/product/smallest/)** and the **[Lumi](/en/product/lumi/)** in Paris.

## Don't miss: the Laboratoire Temps Espace

On the Paris Observatory booth, the **Laboratoire Temps Espace (LTE)** will present its books, its calculation forms, its virtual reality app *La Terre est ronde* and *Eclipseop*, a web app to plan your observation of the total solar eclipse of August 2, 2027. Its researchers are also giving talks (in French):

- **Friday, November 13, 1:30pm**: *Occultation campaigns observed in France*, by Josselin Desmars
- **Sunday, November 15, 10:30am**: *API ProAm: results and future programs*, by Vincent Robert
- **Sunday, November 15, 11:30am**: *Airborne astronomy: chasing shooting stars*, by Jérémie Vaubaillon
- **Sunday, November 15, 12:30pm**: *Join RAPAS: hunting astro alerts*, by Thierry Midavaine and William Thuillot
- **Sunday, November 15, 1:45pm**: *Space detectives: searching for life elsewhere in the universe*, by Jean-Luc Margot

## Practical information

- **Dates:** Friday, November 13 to Sunday, November 15, 2026
- **Hours:** Friday 10am to 8pm, Saturday 10am to 7pm, Sunday 9am to 5:30pm
- **Location:** Cité des sciences et de l'industrie, 30 avenue Corentin-Cariou, 75019 Paris (metro line 7, Porte de la Villette). The show is on levels -1 and -2 of the Congress Center.
- **Prices:** €18 for 1 day, €25 for 2 days, €33 for 3 days (reduced rate for AFA members and Universcience subscribers)
