Proud moment: **La 3ème Dimension** is one of the examples featured in **["Vivre de l'impression 3D"](https://www.eyrolles.com/Loisirs/Livre/vivre-de-l-impression-3d-9782416024559/)** (*Making a living from 3D printing*), the new book by **Mathilde Berchon**, published by Eyrolles in September 2026.

![The book Vivre de l'impression 3D by Mathilde Berchon](/assets/Mathilde1.jpg)

## A guide to starting a 3D printing business

Mathilde Berchon is a key figure in French 3D printing. She wrote the first French book on the subject in 2013, followed by *Le grand livre de l'impression 3D*. Through [FuturFab](https://www.futurfab.fr/), she helps makers, designers, and inventors make a living from digital fabrication.

Subtitled *"Launching and growing your 3D printing business"*, the book covers every step of building an activity: from the initial idea to a catalog of products or services, through business model and marketing. It's built around concrete examples from 3D printing entrepreneurs.

## Designing a consumer telescope with 3D printing

In the chapter *"Finding the right idea"*, a sidebar is dedicated to the workshop. It tells how the first model, the **Smallest**, grew out of a personal need: an instrument lighter and easier to carry than classic telescopes. For almost two years, I refined it (materials, assemblies, user experience, ease of setup). Then came **Lumi**, the educational build-it-yourself refractor designed for kids and beginners.

![The sidebar about La 3ème Dimension and the Lumi refractor, page 92 of the book](/assets/mathilde2.jpg)

3D printing is at the heart of this approach: it makes it possible to prototype quickly, test, and then produce complex parts in small batches right in the workshop.
