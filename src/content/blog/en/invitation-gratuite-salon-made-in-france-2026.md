The **Made in France Show (MIF Expo)** is coming up: from **November 12 to 15, 2026**, find **La 3ème Dimension** in Paris, Porte de Versailles, within the Craft Village.

Entry costs €10 on site, but it's **free with an invitation**. Just register online in advance:

👉 **[Get your free invitation here](https://www.mifexpo.fr/espace-invitations/la-3eme-dimension-997256/)**

## How it works

1. Click the link above to open the MIF Expo invitation page.
2. Fill in the registration form.
3. Show your invitation at the entrance of the show.

⚠️ Don't buy tickets from a street vendor: they won't be accepted at the entrance.

## Practical information

- **Dates:** Thursday, November 12 to Sunday, November 15, 2026
- **Hours:** 10am to 7pm Thursday to Saturday, 10am to 6pm on Sunday
- **Location:** Paris, Porte de Versailles, **Hall 7.2**, Craft Village

At the booth, you'll be able to try the **Smallest**, the **Lumi**, and the workshop's other instruments, and chat about astronomy and 3D printing.

To learn more about our participation, read [La 3ème Dimension at the Made in France Show 2026](/en/blog/salon-made-in-france-2026).

See you in Paris!
