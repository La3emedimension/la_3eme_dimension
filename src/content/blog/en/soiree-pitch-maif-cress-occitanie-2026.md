Good news: **La 3ème Dimension** is one of the **20 projects selected** to pitch at the **MAIF / CRESS Occitanie Pitch Night** (*Grande Soirée Pitch*), on **Wednesday, September 30, 2026** at Halle Tropisme in Montpellier.

## The MAIF Vitrines Engagées

Organized by French insurer **MAIF**, with support from **CRESS Occitanie** (the regional chamber for the social and solidarity economy) and **Halle Tropisme**, the evening will select the first partners of the **Vitrines Engagées** (*Engaged Storefronts*) program in Montpellier.

The call was open to associations, companies, artisans, startups, entrepreneurs, and social and solidarity economy players in Occitanie. After a pre-selection, each shortlisted candidate presents their project on stage.

Winning projects will receive:

- a 3-month visibility space in a MAIF branch;
- dedicated communication support;
- the opportunity to host workshops, talks, or demonstrations;
- access to a network of committed local players.

## What I'll be pitching

I'll present the workshop and its approach: designing and making simple, portable, and affordable astronomical instruments in Occitanie with 3D printing, like the **Smallest** and **Lumi**, the educational build-it-yourself refractor.

A storefront in a branch would be a great opportunity to introduce astronomy to a wide audience, with demonstrations and workshops around the instruments.

## Come and support the projects

The evening is open to the public, with registration:

- **Date:** Wednesday, September 30, 2026, 6:30pm to 10pm
- **Location:** Halle Tropisme, 121 rue Fontcouverte, 34070 Montpellier
- **Registration:** [evening registration form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/AM4PrxhsqQ?origin=lprLink)

If you're around Montpellier, come and cheer us on!
