On November 11, 2026, radio station **RTS** published an article by Corentin Aubry about **La 3ème Dimension**, as part of its series on Occitanie companies exhibiting at the [Made in France show](/en/blog/salon-made-in-france-2026/), November 12–15 in Paris.

## What the article covers

The article starts from a simple observation: it is hard to find a telescope that is both capable and easy to carry on a hike or a bike ride. It then traces the workshop's story: a first wooden prototype, then 3D printing for light, modular parts, and finally the presentation at the Pint of Science festival that launched the project.

It presents the three instruments:

- the **[Smallest](/en/product/smallest/)**, a compact Newtonian telescope that folds in on itself, with removable carbon tubes, designed for amateurs who want precision in a travel bag;
- the **[Picco](/en/product/picco/)**, a lighter version currently being finalized;
- the **[Lumi educational refractor kit](/en/product/lumi/)**, described as the general public's favorite: a 50 mm educational refractor you build like a construction set, in half an hour on your own or an hour as a family, before observing the Moon, Jupiter's moons or star clusters.

The article also mentions the retailer [La Clef des Étoiles](https://laclefdesetoiles.com/) in Plaisance-du-Touch near Toulouse, and the exhibition of the instruments in Montpellier from December 3, as part of [Maif's Vitrines Engagées program](/en/blog/soiree-pitch-maif-cress-occitanie-2026/).
