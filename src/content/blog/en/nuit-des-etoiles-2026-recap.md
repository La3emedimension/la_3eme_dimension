On Friday, August 7, 2026, we were at the **Cité de l'espace** in Toulouse for the **Nuit des Étoiles**, on the Belvédère d'Ariane, running our "Build your own telescope" workshop.

A lovely evening, with curious visitors of all ages assembling their own refractor to discover how optics work, clear skies, and the Perseids as a backdrop. Thanks to everyone who stopped by, and to the Cité de l'espace team for hosting us!
