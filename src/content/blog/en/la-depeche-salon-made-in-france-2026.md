On November 9, 2026, regional newspaper **La Dépêche du Midi** published an article by Éric Théron about **La 3ème Dimension**, a few days before the [Made in France show](/en/blog/salon-made-in-france-2026/) (MIF Expo), November 12–15 in Paris, Porte de Versailles.

## What the article covers

The journalist visited Verdun-sur-Garonne, in the room that serves as office, workshop and meeting room while waiting for "the cabin at the bottom of the garden". The article looks back at how the project started: a first telescope received as a gift, the first sky photos, a wooden prototype, then a first competition and the Pint of Science festival.

It presents the **Smallest**, the compact travel telescope, and the **[Lumi educational refractor kit](/en/product/lumi/)** with its pastel colors:

> "Many people think astronomy is boring. The colors help make it accessible to everyone."

The article also points out that the Lumi is not a toy, that it must never be used to look at the Sun, and that it takes between fifteen minutes and an hour to assemble depending on your DIY skills.

Finally, it mentions a few milestones of the year: trade shows, a talk in front of 5,000 people at the Nuit des étoiles, features in *Ciel & Espace* magazine, and meetings with leading figures of amateur astronomy.
