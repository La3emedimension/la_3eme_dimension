It's been a recurring request from the community: the **open-source Smallest** is finally available on **[MakerWorld](https://makerworld.com/fr/models/2247341-smallest-telescope-kit-for-150-750)**! The kit files for 150/750 optics can be downloaded for free, alongside [Printables](https://www.printables.com/@La3emedimens_2102135) where they were already published.

<a class="button" href="https://makerworld.com/fr/models/2247341-smallest-telescope-kit-for-150-750">See the Smallest on MakerWorld</a>

## Why MakerWorld?

MakerWorld brings together a huge maker community, especially around Bambu Lab printers. Publishing the Smallest there lets even more people discover the project, print their own telescope, and share their makes and observing feedback.

## What you'll find: the Smallest V1

One important note: the version published on MakerWorld is the **Smallest V1**, the original prototype. It's the design that proved the concept (a compact 150/750 Newtonian that fits in cabin luggage and reuses the optics from an off-the-shelf tube) and built the community around the project.

It's still a great starting point to understand how a telescope works and to experiment, but it **isn't as refined as the commercial version** and **doesn't include the updates** made since.

## Open-source version vs. commercial version

Since that V1, the Smallest has evolved a lot thanks to field feedback and many nights under the stars:

- **Mechanical refinements** for more rigidity and smoother assembly
- **Carbon tubes and a removable counterweight** on the ready-to-use version
- **Printed in carbon-fiber-filled PETG** for a light, sturdy structure
- **Checked industrial optics** and a 2-year warranty on the complete version

Both paths make sense: print the open-source V1 to discover the project and start the adventure, or go with the [commercial version in the shop](/en/product/smallest/) to get all the improvements right away.
