Heading for **Occitanie**! At the **Made in France Show (MIF Expo)**, from **November 12 to 15, 2026**, **26 craft businesses** selected by the Chambers of Trades and Crafts (CMA) share the same booth in the **Craft Village**: **booth D21**, Hall 7.2.

La 3ème Dimension is one of them. Here are the artisans you'll find alongside us.

## Fashion and accessories

- **[Les Seins Nus](https://lesseinsnus.com)**: women's clothing made from recycled textiles
- **[Danielle Engel](https://www.danielleengel.com/)**: women's ready-to-wear
- **[Ana Gold](https://www.anagold.fr)**: jewelry and decorations adorned with feathers
- **[La Danse des Cuirs](https://ladansedescuirs.com/wordpress/)**: handmade bags and accessories
- **[Maison Maos](https://www.maisonmaos.fr)**: textile accessories
- **[Elialys – Artisans des sens](https://www.elialys.fr/)**: fragrance creations

## Home and decor

- **[Diptyque audio](https://www.diptyqueaudio.com/)**: high-fidelity flat speakers
- **[FLIP DESIGN BOIS](https://flipdesign.fr/)**: solid wood furniture
- **[Gaga de Déco](https://www.gagadedeco.fr)**: custom chairs, lampshades, cushions and curtains
- **[Lossia, l'éclat des sens](https://www.lossia.fr)**: scented candles
- **[Mystère Anima](https://www.mystereanima.com)**: wooden puzzles and decorative objects
- **[Pyrénées Candles](https://www.pyreneescandles.fr)**: soy wax candles

## Cosmetics, knives and local produce

- **[Labo cosmétique Douceur des Neiges](https://douceurdesneiges.com/fr/)**: natural and organic cosmetics
- **[LeLozère](https://lelozere.fr/)**: knives inspired by the Lozère region
- **[Foie Gras Jacquin](https://foiegras-jacquin.com/)**: duck and goose foie gras, confits and rillettes
- **[L'Atelier du Cerf](https://latelierducerf.fr/)**: Ariège knives and sheaths
- **[Maison Argentin](https://maison-argentin.fr)**: butcher and charcuterie products
- **[La Table Noire Eperluette](https://latablenoireeperluette.com)**: black garlic and slow-aged products

## Drinks and sweets

- **[Foliz Kombucha](https://www.folizkombucha.fr/)**: organic, refreshing, alcohol-free drinks
- **[Koso](https://koso.sumupstore.com/)**: raw fermented syrup
- **[Loco Loco](https://www.liqueurslocoloco.com)**: original fruity liqueurs
- **[De la Fève au Palais](https://www.delafeveaupalais.com/)**: chocolates and sweets with no added sugar
- **[Les Merveilles de Méli](https://merveilles2meli.com/)**: jams and fruit jellies
- **[Chocolaterie du Nouveau Monde](https://www.chocolateriedunouveaumonde.com/)**: chocolates

## Science and nature

- **[La 3ème Dimension](https://la3emedim.fr/)**: telescopes and astronomical instruments
- **[La Mésange Verte / Ecopiège®](https://lamesangeverte.com)**: trap collar against processionary caterpillars

## Practical information

- **Dates:** Thursday, November 12 to Sunday, November 15, 2026
- **Hours:** 10am to 7pm Thursday to Saturday, 10am to 6pm on Sunday
- **Location:** Paris, Porte de Versailles Exhibition Center, **Hall 7.2**, Craft Village, **booth D21**
- **Price:** free with an invitation, €10 on site
