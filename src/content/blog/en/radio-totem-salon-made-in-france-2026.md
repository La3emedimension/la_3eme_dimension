On November 12, 2026, the opening day of the [Made in France show](/en/blog/salon-made-in-france-2026/), regional radio station **Radio Totem** published an article by Johan Gesrel about **La 3ème Dimension** and its 3D-printed instruments.

## What the article covers

The article looks back at the founding of the workshop in April 2025 in Verdun-sur-Garonne, with one goal: making astronomy more accessible. Every part is designed on a computer and then 3D printed, and the instruments are delivered as kits to assemble at home.

> "I wanted to be able to do astronomy anywhere, anytime, easily."

It then presents the **[Lumi educational refractor kit](/en/product/lumi/)**: an instrument for beginners that takes fifteen minutes to an hour to assemble. Building it shows how light travels through the instrument, and it then allows real observations: lunar craters, Saturn's rings, Jupiter and its moons, star clusters and nebulae.

The **[Smallest](/en/product/smallest/)**, a compact Newtonian telescope, is presented as the instrument for going further.

The article also mentions made-to-order production, exchanges with astronomy clubs and amateur astronomers, and the show alongside other Tarn-et-Garonne companies such as the Crambes hat makers from Caussade and Diptyque Audio from Montauban.
